## Reflection
Two or three sentences: what made your prompts better, and one place you had to
correct or reject what the agent produced.

My prompts got better when I pointed the agent at an exact source and told it to verify or diagnose before touching code. Asking it to re-check my older Gemini prompts also caught a lot of outdated instructions.  It forgot to request review from my partner and to link a PR to its issue. And I rejected its original explanation for hiding Create Ticket from admins once my reviewer proved it was wrong.
